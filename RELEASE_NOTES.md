# NodeGate 0.1.0-beta.1

## English

The first NodeGate beta brings per-vehicle access control to Cities: Skylines II road and rail nodes. Open the top-left NodeGate panel, choose exact vehicle types, and apply them directly to a node.

This beta needs real-save testing. Exact restrictions are hard gates; affected vehicles may wait rather than reroute. Back up important saves and report reproducible issues.

## 中文

NodeGate 首个 Beta 版为《都市：天际线 II》的道路和轨道节点带来精确车型通行控制。点击左上角 NodeGate 面板，选择具体车型后即可直接应用到节点。

本版本仍需真实存档测试。精确限制采用硬性闸门，被拦车辆可能等待而非绕行。请先备份重要存档，并提交可复现的问题。
